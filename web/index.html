⚡ Stroom

Add Stroom to your home screen

It opens full screen like an app, and still shows the last prices when you’re offline.

iPhone

  1. Tap Share in Safari’s toolbar. If you don’t see it, tap More first.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen — scroll down the list if needed.
  3. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Tap Menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install.
